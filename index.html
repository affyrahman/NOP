Typing Shortcuts (Instant Speaker Tag Insertion) New & Fast

Type @c, @p, or @d1 followed by Space or Enter to automatically generate the speaker tag and immediately start typing:

@c → COURT: Court
@p → PROSECUTION: Prosecution
@d1 → D1: Defendant 1
@d2 → D2: Defendant 2
@dc → DC: Defense Counsel
@i → INTERP: Interpreter
@w → WITNESS: Witness
@o → ORDER: Court Order
@t → [HH:MM] Timestamp
